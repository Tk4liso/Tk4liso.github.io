Time Series Forecasting

Tracking the 200% PM Pollution Spike

A rigid spreadsheet doesn't inspire urban planning changes. By applying ARIMA modeling to historical data, I mapped the trajectory of airborne particulate matter, visualizing a critical 200% pollution spike. To maximize impact, I deployed these findings as an interactive Shiny app, empowering non-technical stakeholders to explore the data dynamically.

Behavioral NLP Metrics

Mapping Conversational Dynamics

Standard if/else heuristics often struggle with the ambiguity of human conversation. Instead of rigid rules, my thesis utilized a Mamdani-type fuzzy logic layer to calculate behavioral metrics. This 2D matrix visualizes some of the results, plotting the average stress versus behavioral interest of two users in a WhatsApp conversation, demonstrating how the system quantifies abstract communicative patterns.

Unsupervised Learning

Dimensionality Reduction & Risk Profiling

In machine learning, raw predictive power isn't enough; interpretability is key. I utilized PCA to simplify behavioral survey data before executing K-means and PAM clustering to identify distinct user segments. This segmentation directly informed our CART decision trees, achieving a strict Kappa agreement metric of 0.93.
